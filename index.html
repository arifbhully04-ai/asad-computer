        Our History

        Asad Computer & Training Center was founded in Bhully Bazar to bring practical computer education to the young people of Thakurgaon.

        Starting with a small lab and a handful of learners, the center now offers basic computer courses, office applications, graphic design and other skill training for students and job seekers in the region.

        Our aim has stayed the same: affordable training, hands-on practice, and a certificate that helps students find work or start their own.
